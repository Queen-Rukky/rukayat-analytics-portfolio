# Rukayat Analytics Portfolio

Case studies in SQL, Power BI, and Excel, built to show how I turn messy business data into decisions a stakeholder can act on.

## About Me

I'm Rukayat Adebisi Obanor, a Data Analyst and CRM Administrator based in Lagos, Nigeria. I teach Data Analytics at Digital Skills Nigeria, and I work across SQL, Power BI, Python, Excel, and Zoho CRM. My background is in theatre and design (MA Design and Management, BA Theatre Arts, University of Lagos), which shapes how I frame and present a dashboard: every project here is built to tell a story, not just display numbers.

## Projects

| Project | Problem | Tools | Highlights |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Ecommerce Sales Analysis](./ecommerce-sales-analysis) | A beauty and skincare brand's raw order data was too messy to trust for reporting | Excel, Pivot Tables | Cleaned 250+ orders, built a revenue dashboard by product, channel, and country |
